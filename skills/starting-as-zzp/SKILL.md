# Starting as a zzp'er

## What it is

A zzp'er (zelfstandige zonder personeel) is a sole trader, usually an eenmanszaak. Starting one touches four
bodies: KVK (the trade register), the Belastingdienst (VAT and income tax), the pension and insurance market,
and, through the client, the rules on false self-employment. This skill walks through them in order, as of
**2026**. Check dates and amounts again before relying on them.

VAT itself (rates, KOR, invoices, returns) is in `dutch-vat-zzp`. Income tax for someone who keeps a job and
earns on the side is in `dutch-tax`.

## Step 1: must the owner register at KVK?

KVK says someone must register when all three criteria are met:

1. they supply products or services **independently**, or are preparing to (for example, materials bought);
2. they ask a **price** with which they earn money, so not a hobby that only costs money;
3. they supply **regularly** to others than just family or friends.

In doubt, KVK asks four more questions: does the owner invest time or money or hire staff; work regularly
and for longer; have or seek several clients; decide how, where and when they work? All yes: register.
Still unsure: the KVK Adviesteam can talk it through.

Not registering while meeting the criteria breaks the Handelsregisterwet and is an offence under the Wet op
de economische delicten. So say it plainly when the criteria are met.

## Step 2: registering

- **When**: from 1 week before the start, and at the latest 1 week after starting.
- **Prepare**: a business name (check it with KVK's Naamchecker; it may not mislead or copy someone else's
  name), the legal form, a description of the activities and the sector.
- **How**: fill in the online form, book an appointment, and go to a KVK office with a **valid ID**. Without
  an appointment and ID it is not possible. The KVK number is given at the visit.
- **Cost**: a one-time **€ 85.15**, invoiced by e-mail after the visit. It cannot be paid in cash, and it
  is tax-deductible for an entrepreneur.
- **Public data**: name, contact details, trade names, activities, SBI code and visiting and postal address
  are public in the Handelsregister. The phone number is shielded. KVK names limited ways to shield the
  visiting address: for an eenmanszaak, registering a separate postal address, and a request in case of a
  threat. Tell the owner this before they register their home address.

KVK assigns an SBI code for the activities. The owner should check it now and then, because a wrong code can
mean missing a permit or subsidy.

## Step 3: what the Belastingdienst does after

The owner does **not** register separately with the Belastingdienst. KVK passes the data on. For an
eenmanszaak the registration uses the owner's BSN, and the Belastingdienst derives the omzetbelastingnummer
from it. Processing takes **at most 10 working days**; then a letter follows about the taxes that apply.

The Belastingdienst decides two separate things:

- **Ondernemer for VAT**: then the owner gets an **omzetbelastingnummer** and a **btw-identificatienummer**.
  The ob-nummer contains the BSN and must never go on invoices or a website; see `dutch-vat-zzp`.
- **Ondernemer for income tax**: judged on facts such as profit, independence, capital, time spent, several
  clients, visibility (website, social media, letterhead), business risk and liability for debts. Only then
  do the entrepreneur deductions below apply. Otherwise the income is "overig werk" (see `dutch-tax`).

Someone who does not have to register at KVK may still have to file VAT, and then signs up with the form
"Opgaaf startende onderneming". Below a turnover of **€ 2,200** a calendar year, a small business that is not
in the KVK register does not have to sign up for VAT at all.

## Step 4: the hours criterion (urencriterium)

The zelfstandigenaftrek and the startersaftrek need the urencriterium (art. 3.6 Wet IB 2001). The owner
usually meets it when:

1. they spend at least **1,225 hours** in the calendar year on their business; and
2. they spend **more time on the business than on other work**, such as a job. This second condition does
   not apply if they were not an entrepreneur in one or more of the 5 previous years.

Points that go wrong:

- **No pro rata.** Starting on 1 July still means 1,225 hours in that year.
- **All business hours count**, not only billed ones: quotes, bookkeeping, building the website. Being
  merely available (on call without work) does not count.
- **Proof.** The owner must make the hours plausible, for example with an agenda, quotes, hour sheets and
  invoices. Suggest keeping a simple hours log from day one.
- **With a job next to it**: condition 2 is the hard one once the starter years are over. Say so early.

What it brings in 2026 (art. 3.76 and 3.79a):

| Item | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Zelfstandigenaftrek, below AOW age at the start of the year | € 1,200 |
| Startersaftrek, on top, if not an entrepreneur in one or more of the 5 previous years and the zelfstandigenaftrek was used at most twice in those years | € 2,123 |
| MKB-winstvrijstelling, on profit after the ondernemersaftrek | 12.7% |

In the top income tax bracket these deductions save less than the top rate (art. 2.10).

## Step 5: insurance and pension, at a high level

**Disability (AOV).** The government plans a **mandatory disability insurance for the self-employed**, with a benefit when long illness
means they can no longer earn the minimum wage. As of 2026 it is a proposal: both chambers still have to
decide and the start date is not known. Until then insuring is the owner's own choice. Do not recommend a
product; lay out the risk and suggest an independent adviser.

**Pension.** A zzp'er does not build up pension automatically. They get AOW like everyone else. Options the
government lists:

- **Continue the old employer's scheme**: tell the pension provider within **9 months** after the job ended;
  it must start within 15 months. The premium is deductible for at most 10 years.
- **Mandatory schemes**: some professions and sectors have a pension fund that self-employed people must join.
  Check it before assuming there is none.
- **Lijfrente or banksparen**: premiums are deductible within the jaarruimte and reserveringsruimte (the
  Belastingdienst has a calculator); the payouts are taxed later in box 1.
- **Own savings or investments** (box 3), or a voluntary **collective scheme** for zzp'ers.
- The fiscale oudedagsreserve (FOR) stopped in 2023; no new build-up.

Mijnpensioenoverzicht.nl shows AOW and pensions from past employers, but not what the owner builds up as
self-employed.

## Step 6: false self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid), as of 2026

Schijnzelfstandigheid means working as a zzp'er while, by the rules, it is really employment, for example
because the zzp'er cannot decide when and how the work is done. Client and zzp'er must prevent it together.

How the Belastingdienst enforces it (from its own page):

- Until 1 January 2025 there was a **handhavingsmoratorium**. That has ended; normal rules apply again.
- The Belastingdienst no longer gives warnings first. It can **directly impose corrections and additional
  wage tax assessments** (naheffingsaanslagen loonheffingen) on the client.
- **Fines**: none in 2025. **From 1 January 2026 vergrijpboetes** (for intent or gross negligence) are
  possible. **No verzuimboetes in 2026.**
- Assessments go **back to 1 January 2025 at most**, unless there was bad faith or an earlier instruction
  was ignored: then up to 5 years.
- The way of working can change over time, so check the relationship again regularly.
- The Belastingdienst no longer reviews new **modelovereenkomsten**. An approved one that is in use may be
  used **until the end of 2029**, but only if the work in practice matches it.

**New from 31 December 2026: a presumption of employment at a low hourly rate.** Under a new article 7:610aa
of the Civil Code, someone who works for another at a rate of at most a set hourly amount is presumed to work
under an employment contract. Ondernemersplein gives the amount as **€ 38 per hour** (indexed with the
minimum wage). The zzp'er can invoke it; the client must then prove there is no employment contract. It does
not apply when the client is a private person not acting in a business or profession. The law was published
in the Staatsblad in June 2026 and starts on 31 December 2026.

Tools to check an assignment: the SZW keuzehulp, the Webmodule Beoordeling Arbeidsrelatie, and the
Belastingdienst's Toelichting Beoordeling arbeidsrelaties. Broader new rules for zzp'ers are being worked on;
no date is known.

## Step 7: record keeping

- Keep **all** business records, on paper or digital, for **7 years**; sometimes longer.
- Invoices have their own rules; see `dutch-vat-zzp`.
- Keep proof of **costs and hours from before registration** (the aanloopfase): they can often go in the
  income tax return. VAT on start-up costs can be reclaimed within 6 months after the year they were made,
  so sign up as an entrepreneur as soon as possible.

## How to work with it

1. Establish the facts: what the owner will sell, to whom, how many clients, next to a job or not, start date.
2. Walk through the KVK criteria with them. If all are met, give the registration window as dates.
3. Prepare the KVK form and the appointment with them; booking and signing wait for their yes (see
   `acting-on-behalf`).
4. From the first week: an hours log, a folder for receipts, and a calendar entry for the VAT deadlines.
5. Before a first assignment: read the contract with them against the schijnzelfstandigheid points and the
   hourly rate. Draft questions for the client; the owner sends them.
6. Put AOV and pension on the list as decisions to make, with an adviser if they want one (see
   `making-decisions`).

## What not to claim

- Do not tell the owner they are or are not an entrepreneur for income tax, or that an assignment is or is not
  schijnzelfstandigheid. The Belastingdienst, and in a dispute the court, decides.
- Do not promise the zelfstandigenaftrek before the year's hours are in.
- Do not present the mandatory AOV as law in force, or give its premium as final.
- Do not choose a legal form, an insurer or a pension product for the owner.

## Where this stops

This follows KVK and Ondernemersplein on registering, the Belastingdienst on registration, entrepreneurship
for income tax, the urencriterium, enforcement on employment relationships and record keeping, the Wet
inkomstenbelasting 2001 as in force in 2026, Ondernemersplein on false self-employment, the planned
disability insurance and pensions for zzp'ers, and the Staatsblad text of the hourly rate presumption. It
does not cover a bv or partnership, hiring staff, permits, or foreign businesses. For those, or when a
contract or real money depends on it, point to KVK, the BelastingTelefoon, or an accountant or lawyer.
