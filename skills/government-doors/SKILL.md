# Government doors in the Netherlands

## What it is

Dutch government services are spread over many organisations, each with its own site and login. Most use
**DigiD** to log in. This skill says which door handles what, where people get help in person, and the one
correct way for someone else to act for the owner: **DigiD Machtigen**. It is a map, not the rules of each
scheme; for those, go to the organisation's own site.

## The first rule: never someone else's DigiD

DigiD's own advice: never give your DigiD login details to someone else; use DigiD Machtigen instead. That
holds for the assistant too. Do not ask for, store or use the owner's DigiD username, password, codes or app.
If the owner offers them, say no and explain machtigen (below). The owner logs in themselves.

## Which door for what

| Matter | Door | Online |
|---|---|---|
| Digital post from government | **MijnOverheid**, Berichtenbox | mijn.overheid.nl, with DigiD |
| Taxes, like the income tax return | **Belastingdienst** | belastingdienst.nl |
| Zorgtoeslag, huurtoeslag, kinderopvangtoeslag, kindgebonden budget | **Dienst Toeslagen** | belastingdienst.nl/toeslagen, log in |
| Registration of who lives in the municipality (BRP) | **Gemeente** | the municipality's site |
| Passport, ID card, driving licence | **Gemeente** (driving licence renewal can also be done online at the RDW) | municipality / rdw.nl |
| Bijstand, Participatiewet, Wmo support at home, jeugdhulp | **Gemeente** (bijstand is applied for via werk.nl; the gemeente decides) | municipality / werk.nl |
| WW, WIA, WAO, Wajong, finding work | **UWV** | Mijn UWV |
| AOW, kinderbijslag, nabestaanden- en wezenuitkering, AIO, PGB | **SVB** | Mijn SVB, Mijn PGB |
| Student finance, student travel product, repaying study debt, diplomas | **DUO** | duo.nl, log in |
| Own contribution (eigen bijdrage) for Wmo and Wlz care, health insurance premium arrears | **CAK** | hetcak.nl |
| Registration number (kenteken), transferring a vehicle, APK date | **RDW** | rdw.nl |
| Registering, changing or ending a business, trade register extract | **KVK** | kvk.nl |
| Disputes about rent | **Huurcommissie** | see `huurrecht` |

When a letter arrives, the sender is the door: questions about a letter go to the organisation that sent it,
not to MijnOverheid.

## MijnOverheid and the Berichtenbox

- The Berichtenbox is a **digital letterbox**. It only receives; you cannot send messages through it.
- Many organisations are connected (for example the Belastingdienst, municipalities, the Kadaster, UWV), but
  not all, and each organisation decides which post goes digital. Some also send paper.
- The owner chooses which organisations may send there (Instellingen, Organisaties Berichtenbox), and can
  turn on e-mail notifications and reminders for unread post.
- Something deleted from the trash cannot be recovered; ask the sender to send it again.

For the assistant: the Berichtenbox itself is not reachable without the owner's DigiD. Suggest the owner
turns on e-mail notifications, and that they forward or show you a letter they want help with. Then help
read it: who sent it, what is asked, by when, what happens if nothing is done (see `personal-triage`).

## DigiD Machtigen: acting for someone else

A machtiging (authorisation) lets a person or a business (for example a family member, a social worker, a tax
adviser) handle government matters for someone. It is given:

- for a **specific task**, like a tax return, and
- for a **fixed period**, like a year.

Two ways to set it up, both at machtigen.digid.nl:

1. **Iemand machtigen**: the person who wants help requests a code for the helper, then gives the helper the
   code and their BSN. Without a DigiD they can call the DigiD helpdesk (088 123 65 55); the code then comes
   by letter to their BRP address.
2. **Gemachtigd worden**: the helper requests a code with the other person's BSN. The code is sent by post to
   that person's home address, and **that person must hand it over personally**. So consent always comes from
   the person themselves.

The helper then logs in with **their own** DigiD and enters the code and BSN. Active, expired and revoked
authorisations are in "Mijn machtigingen", where the person can end one at any time or change its end date.
Abroad or in Caribbean Netherlands, the letter cannot arrive; the machtiging is then arranged online at
machtigen.digid.nl.

**Berichtenbox by machtiging:** chosen per organisation and per part; there is no single authorisation for all
post. The authorised person sees only post from those organisations and may read, archive, delete, and forward
it to their own registered e-mail. They do not see the owner's MijnOverheid account or personal data.

**When may someone act for another?** For this government route: when the person has given a DigiD machtiging
for that task and that period. Offer it; do not push it. If the person cannot consent at all (for example
because of illness), a machtiging is not the route: point to Het Juridisch Loket or a lawyer. This is practice
advice, not a rule from DigiD.

## Help in person

- **Informatiepunt Digitale Overheid (IDO)**: free help, for everyone, with using the digital government. Mostly
  in local libraries, more and more also in community buildings. Questions about toeslagen, taxes, DigiD, AOW,
  work, benefits, driving licence and care. Most questions are about requesting and activating DigiD and the
  DigiD app. For complex or personal questions they refer to local partners.
- **The organisation itself**: phone and contact forms on its own site. DigiD
  helpdesk: 088 123 65 55.

## How to work with it

1. Find out what the matter is and who sent the letter. Use the table to name the door.
2. Give the owner the organisation's own site and what they will need (DigiD, BSN, the letter).
3. Put any deadline from the letter in the agenda (`planning-a-day`).
4. The owner logs in and submits. Anything that reaches the organisation in the owner's name waits for their
   explicit yes (`acting-on-behalf`).
5. If the owner struggles, suggest the nearest IDO or a machtiging for a trusted person.

## What not to claim

- Do not say you logged in, checked a status or submitted anything; you cannot without the owner's DigiD, and
  you must not use it.
- Do not decide eligibility for a benefit or allowance. Name the door and let the organisation decide.
- Do not follow a link in an e-mail or text that asks for DigiD details. Go to the organisation's site
  directly (practice advice).
- Do not treat this map as complete: many more bodies exist (CBR, CIZ, CJIB, and others).

## Where this stops

Based on digid.nl (Machtigen), mijn.overheid.nl (Berichtenbox and machtigen), digitaleoverheid.nl (IDO),
Rijksoverheid (tasks of a municipality; UWV in the contact guide) and the sites of the Belastingdienst
(Toeslagen), SVB, DUO, CAK, RDW and KVK. Services and login options change; check the organisation's own site.
It does not cover business logins (eHerkenning) or legal representation.
