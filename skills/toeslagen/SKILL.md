# Toeslagen

## What it is

Toeslagen are income-dependent allowances paid by **Dienst Toeslagen**, part of the Belastingdienst. The shared
rules are in the Algemene wet inkomensafhankelijke regelingen (Awir, BWBR0018472); each allowance has its own
law and conditions. The four:

| Allowance | For |
|---|---|
| **Zorgtoeslag** | help paying the Dutch health insurance |
| **Huurtoeslag** | help paying the rent of a home |
| **Kinderopvangtoeslag** | part of the cost of registered childcare while parents work or study |
| **Kindgebonden budget** | a contribution to the costs of children under 18 |

The figures here are for **2026**. They change every year: check the Dienst Toeslagen page for the year in
question. The quickest check of the owner's own situation is the official **proefberekening** (trial
calculation) on the Dienst Toeslagen site.

## The one idea to get across: it is an advance

A toeslag is a **voorschot**, an advance calculated on an **estimate** of the year's income. After the year,
Dienst Toeslagen takes the real income, known once the income tax return is filed, and makes a **definitieve
berekening**. If the real income was higher, too much was paid and it must be paid back. If the income of a
partner or co-resident changed, the toeslag is recalculated too. Almost every repayment comes from this.

The income that counts is the **toetsingsinkomen**: the income figure for that year (art. 8 Awir), which
comes from the income tax. So a new side income or a raise feeds straight into the toeslag. See `dutch-tax`.

## Who can get what, in 2026

**Zorgtoeslag**

- A Dutch health insurance, and 18 or older (apply only after taking out the insurance).
- Income in 2026 at most **€ 40,857**, or **€ 51,142** together with a toeslagpartner.
- Vermogen on **1 January 2026** at most **€ 146,011**, or **€ 184,633** together.

**Huurtoeslag** (2026 rules)

- **There is no maximum rent any more** from 2026. Someone who was refused before because the rent was too
  high may now qualify. Check it again.
- Only the **kale huur** counts from 2026; service charges no longer count.
- The lower amount for young people now applies up to age **21** (it was 23), so 21 and 22 year olds often get
  more than in 2025.
- The home must be a **zelfstandige woonruimte**: its own lockable front door, kitchen and toilet, and since
  1 March 2024 its own shower or bath. A houseboat does not qualify; a holiday home usually not.
- Vermogen on 1 January 2026: at most **€ 38,479 per resident**, **€ 76,958** for partners together. A child
  under 18 living at home counts with the parent.
- The income limit depends on rent, age and household. There is no single number: use the proefberekening.

**Kinderopvangtoeslag**

- The owner and any partner **work** (employed, zzp'er, freelancer or entrepreneur all count), or follow a
  recognised education, a route to work, or a compulsory inburgering course.
- The childcare is registered in the **Landelijk Register Kinderopvang** and has an LRK number. Without it,
  no toeslag.
- Parent and child live at the same address and are both registered there with the municipality.
- The parent always pays an own contribution and must be able to show the childcare was paid, within 6
  months after the year.
- 2026: households with a joint income up to **€ 56,413** get 96% of the maximum hourly price. The maximum
  hourly prices are **€ 11.23** day care, **€ 9.98** out-of-school care, **€ 8.49** childminder.

**Kindgebonden budget**

- Goes to the parent who has the **kinderbijslag** from the SVB in their name.
- Vermogen on 1 January 2026 at most **€ 146,011**, or **€ 184,633** together.
- There is no single income limit to quote: use the proefberekening.
- Someone who already has one of the other three toeslagen does not need to apply; Dienst Toeslagen writes
  if they qualify. Otherwise apply in Mijn toeslagen.

## Applying

- Apply in **Mijn toeslagen**, with DigiD. A partner co-signs the application (art. 15 Awir).
- **Zorgtoeslag, huurtoeslag and kindgebonden budget** can be requested until **31 December of the following
  year**: for 2025, until 31 December 2026 (art. 15 Awir). With uitstel for the income tax return, the window
  runs until the uitstel date.
- **Kinderopvangtoeslag**: apply as soon as the contract with the childcare is signed, and **within 3
  months** after the childcare starts. Later means lost months. Example from Dienst Toeslagen: care from
  1 May, apply by 31 August; applying in October means toeslag only from 1 July.
- A child needs a BSN before kinderopvangtoeslag can be requested.

## Passing on changes

Changes that affect the amount must be passed on **within 4 weeks**, in Mijn toeslagen. Typical ones:

- income goes up or down, a new job, a side income, a bonus year;
- a partner moves in or out, marriage, separation;
- the rent changes, or the childcare changes;
- vermogen changes around 1 January.

Changes the owner already gives to the municipality, such as moving house, do not need to be passed on again.

## Paying back, and how to avoid it

**Avoiding it** (these follow from how the advance works):

1. Keep the estimated income in Mijn toeslagen close to reality. When a side income starts, update it that
   week, not at the end of the year.
2. Include the partner's income and changes too.
3. Compare the voorschotbeschikking letter with what is really expected. If the estimate is off, change it.
4. In practice, when unsure, an estimate on the high side means less advance now and money back later,
   rather than a debt. That is a choice for the owner, not a rule.

**When a repayment comes**:

- Open Mijn toeslagen and compare the definitieve berekening with the last voorschotbeschikking of that year.
  The difference, usually the income, is the reason.
- Whatever cannot be paid at once can be paid in a **betalingsregeling of up to 24 months**. The owner does
  not have to call: paying the first month's amount before the date in the letter starts it. Since
  1 January 2026 the interest on such an arrangement is **4.3%**.
- If 24 months leaves too little to live on, ask for a **persoonlijke betalingsregeling**.
- The law lets Dienst Toeslagen reclaim less than the full amount when full recovery would be
  disproportionate (art. 26 Awir). That is for serious cases; do not promise it.
- Disagreeing with a decision: **bezwaar within 6 weeks** of the date on the letter.

## Where to get help

- Hulppunten near the owner: welfare organisations, sociaal raadslieden, neighbourhood teams. Bring valid ID.
- The **library**, often with a walk-in hour and an Informatiepunt Digitale Overheid.
- **Geldfit**, free and anonymous, for money worries.
- The **BelastingTelefoon**, or an appointment by phone, video or at a desk.
- Someone the owner trusts, through **DigiD Machtigen**, can arrange things in Mijn toeslagen for them.
- A paid adviser can do it, but the owner stays responsible for the data sent.

## How to work with it

1. Establish the household first: partner, co-residents, children and ages, rent, childcare, vermogen on
   1 January, expected income for the whole year.
2. Point the owner to the proefberekening and go through it together.
3. Put the deadlines in the calendar: the 4 weeks after a change, the 3 months for childcare, 31 December for
   last year's application.
4. Prepare applications and changes with the owner; submitting in Mijn toeslagen waits for their yes (see
   `acting-on-behalf`). Toeslagen can be a worrying subject: go gently (see `calm-and-rest`).

## What not to claim

- Do not say the owner is entitled, or name an amount, without the proefberekening or a letter.
- Do not give limits from memory, or 2026 limits for another year.
- Do not tell the owner to leave out income, a partner or a co-resident. It only leads to paying back later.
- Do not promise that a debt will be reduced or waived.

## Where this stops

This follows the Awir as in force in 2026 and the Dienst Toeslagen pages on the 2026 changes, the conditions
for each allowance, application deadlines, passing on changes, paying back, the betalingsregeling, objections
and help. It does not cover the recovery operation for parents affected by the kinderopvangtoeslag affair,
living or working abroad, co-parenting details, or the harshness clause. For those, or for a large debt,
point to Dienst Toeslagen, a hulppunt, or a debt counsellor.
